Syllogism Golden Rules

1. If statement is Positive, Negative conclusion is always False (in definite cases).
2. "Only a few A are B" means "Some A are B" AND "Some A are NOT B".
3. "Only A is B" means "All B are A" (and B cannot interact with anything else).
